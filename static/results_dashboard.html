Degradation Mechanics Discussion:

Figure 2(a) illustrates a 120-day monitored degradation profile. Under nominal load, THI degrades at a steady rate of $m = 0.12\text{ THI/day}$. As thermal stress surges on Day 80 ($\Theta_H = 128^\circ\text{C}$), the Arrhenius aging acceleration factor $F_{AA}$ spikes to 6.2× normal consumption (Figure 2(b)). This steepens the degradation slope to $m = 0.88\text{ THI/day}$, causing the service date predictor to adjust the required inspection forward to Day 96 (16 days before reaching the critical threshold). Concurrently, solid Kraft paper Degree of Polymerization ($DP$) decays toward 260, safely above the critical tear boundary of 200 (Figure 2(c)).

Winding Hot-Spot: \(\Theta_H = T_{oil} + 18.0 \cdot (I/I_{rated})^{1.6}\)
Arrhenius Factor: \(F_{AA} = \exp\left(\frac{15000}{383.15} - \frac{15000}{\Theta_H + 273.15}\right)\)
Extrapolated Service Date: \(\Delta t_{service} = (\text{THI}_{current} - 70.0) / m\)

Figure 3: Oscillographic Fault Waveform Capture and Hardware Interlock Actuation

Transient Interruption Analysis:

A severe overload fault was induced at $t = 30.0\text{ ms}$, driving load current from 10.0 A to 38.0 A peak. Within 12.0 ms (half an AC cycle at 50 Hz), the STM32 ADC pipeline crossed the trip threshold. The GPIO pulled the optocoupled relay driver LOW, opening physical contacts and fully extinguishing the AC arc at $t = 56.0\text{ ms}$. Total fault interruption time was 26.0 ms (<1.5 AC cycles), preventing winding insulation burnout.

Strict Edge Autonomy Guarantee

Because threshold monitoring and relay tripping execute on the STM32 Cortex-M3 microcontroller, protection operates deterministically even if the LoRa radio is disconnected, the host computer is shut down, or the AI engine encounters an inference delay.

Economic and Operational Feasibility

For rural distribution utilities managing thousands of remote pole-mounted transformers, the proposed system solves the fundamental barrier that has prevented proactive condition-based maintenance: cost. By replacing expensive SCADA transducers and monthly cellular subscription plans with commodity microcontrollers, edge risk fusion, and license-free LoRa radio, utilities can achieve comprehensive automated protection, life prognosis, and AI playbooks at less than 1% of the cost of existing commercial alternatives.
